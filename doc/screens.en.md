# Screens

[← README](../README.en.md) · [Русский](screens.md)

How jRadio looks on an ST7796S 480×320 display with the Standard theme. Other
displays have the same layout, only smaller; the colours change in the theme
settings.

## Home screen

![Home screen: the carousel of sources](screens/main_screen.jpg)

The sources in a carousel - the encoder turns it, a press opens one. The
weather, the clock and the Wi-Fi level are always on top. What the board does
not have is not in the carousel.

## Station list

![The internet radio station list](screens/list_stantions.jpg)

The numbers are the ones the remote's digits dial and the alarm picks.

## FM radio

![FM radio: the frequency, the RDS name, the preset's picture](screens/FM.jpg)

The frequency in large digits, the station's name from RDS, the preset's
picture, the stereo mark and the signal level.

## Folders on a USB drive

![Folders on a USB drive](screens/list_files.jpg)

A USB drive and an SD card are browsed the same way: folders, files, long
names.

## File player

![The player: FLAC at 96 kHz with the album cover](screens/USB_Player.jpg)

The album, the track and the artist from the tags, the cover from the file or
the folder, the format and the bit rate, the VU meter, the position in the
track and the volume.

## Bluetooth

![Bluetooth from a phone](screens/bluetooth.jpg)

Sound from a phone through the jradio-bt module: the track's name and cover
come from the phone, and the player's keys control it.

## Settings

![Settings on the device's screen](screens/setup.jpg)

The settings right on the device, grouped in sections; at the bottom the web
interface's address, and on a press a QR code for a phone.
